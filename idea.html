RESEARCH DESIGN

Digital literacy is more than screen time.

TechPulse measures practical competence, not how often someone uses a phone. Questions are adapted to age context and tagged by skill domain.

01 · Age-adaptive

Each participant receives a question set appropriate to their age bracket: 8–12, 13–19, 20–40, or 50–80.

02 · Domain-based

Every response maps to a measurable competency, allowing researchers to identify specific skill gaps rather than relying on one total score.

03 · Practical

Questions use realistic situations: phishing, search results, cloud files, digital payments, messaging and troubleshooting.

04 · Privacy-aware

The prototype avoids names, phone numbers and exact addresses. Production deployments should use consent, data minimisation and appropriate safeguards.

Five domains

Device & Software

Navigation, apps, files, updates and settings.

Information Literacy

Search, verification, misinformation and sources.

Safety & Security

Passwords, phishing, scams, privacy and MFA.

Communication

Email, messaging, collaboration and digital etiquette.

Digital Tools

Cloud storage, spreadsheets, productivity and coding concepts.

Important: A public online convenience sample should not be described as nationally representative. For formal research, define sampling methods, recruit across relevant regions, pilot the instrument, document consent procedures and report limitations.
